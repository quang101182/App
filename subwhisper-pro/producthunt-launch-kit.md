## Description Product Hunt

Ever spent hours fixing AI-generated subtitles full of phantom words, wrong speakers, and gibberish? You're not alone. Most transcription tools just run Whisper and call it a day.

SubWhisper Pro goes further.

It's a web app that transcribes and translates subtitles using a multi-pass AI pipeline. Instead of a single Whisper pass, it runs AI Cleanup to catch and fix hallucinations — those phantom words and repeated phrases that plague raw Whisper output. Then it translates into 75+ languages using multi-pass AI translation, where each pass refines context, tone, and accuracy.

What makes it different:
• AI Cleanup — automatically detects and removes Whisper hallucinations
• Multi-pass translation — not just word-for-word, but context-aware across passes
• Foreign language detection — auto-identifies the source language
• Works offline as a PWA — your files never leave your device
• Export to SRT, ASS, VTT, TXT, JSON
• €9/month — 3x less than VEED ($24/mo) or Kapwing ($24/mo)

Built by a solo developer, not a VC-funded startup. No bloat, no upsells, just a tool that does one thing really well.

Start with a 14-day free trial. No credit card needed.
